[[Simone de Beauvoir]] rejects the idea that perception can ever come from nowhere. Every act of seeing is shaped by a particular perspective, formed by one's body, history, experience, etc.

This means that bias is not simply an obstacle to clear perception. It is part of the condition under which anything becomes intelligible at all. A person cannot step outside themselves and encounter the world as a newborn consciousness.

The real danger is not having biases, but believing one has transcended them. Claims to total [[objectivity]] often conceal the [[perspective]] they pretend to escape.
