Human existence is fundamentally ambiguous. 

I'm as much a subject for myself (capable of action, free, conscious, mutating) as I am an object for others (looked at, judged by fixed/visible features, reduced to them).
I'm both [[facticity-and-transcendence|facticity and transcendence]], mind and body.
I'm both what I project for my future and what my past projects onto me.
I am me, I am family, I am nation. 

These dualities do not need to be collapsed, figured out, fixed, [[seeing-things-as-puzzles-to-be-solved|solved]]. Doing so is twisted. Being in that "middle" is part of the landscape of our being. Not doing so is [[tension-between-dualities|uncomfortable]]. The question is: [[how-to-live-in-ambiguity|how can one live well without denying this middle]].
