Lately I read my days like a tarot spread — a vibe check here, a sign there, the whole *everything* interpreted. Lisbon feels like somebody that I used to know; we watched *Amélie* and the character of the Rain; everything seems to rhyme. It's the [[everything-is-the-same-is-about-attention|"everything is the same thing"]] reflex again, turned outward onto the calendar.

[[the-unbearable-lightness-of-being|Kundera]] gives me the kinder reading of it. He says we compose our lives, without realising, *according to the laws of beauty*: a chance event becomes a motif, recurs, and takes a permanent place in the composition — the way a theme returns in a piece of music. And he thinks it would be wrong to deprive yourself of this dimension of beauty just because the coincidences aren't "objectively" meaningful.

So I try to hold two things at once. I don't fully trust the fascination — signs are cheap, and the mind is a pattern-pump that will find a face in any wall. But I refuse to deprive myself of it either. The rule I'm settling on: attend to the coincidences as *beauty*, not as *instructions*. Let yourself be moved; don't let yourself be told.
