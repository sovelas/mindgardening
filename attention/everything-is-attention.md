"Everything is the same thing", I keep thinking. Less of a claim about the world than a claim about *me*. It's not that there's objectively something out there stitching these unrelated snippets of life together; it's that the angle I watch them from makes the same few patterns shine, and that shine overshadows almost everything else.

That's humbling, because it means the unity I keep finding is downstream of where I put my attention, not upstream of it. [[history-of-default-settings|My defaults]] decide what counts as a pattern before I ever consciously notice one.

The (tiny) skeptic in me says this is just [[apophenia]] with good lighting — a private self-regurgitating I can stretch over anything. But I guess noticing isn't the same as inventing. It's[[particular<->universal|reaching the universal through the particular]]: I see a general shape precisely because I'm standing somewhere specific.

So this garden is some sort of map of my [[happiness-as-attention|attention]]. Might be the only map I can get.
