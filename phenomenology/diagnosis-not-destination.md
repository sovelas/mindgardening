[[Husserl]]'s late diagnosis in [[the-crisis-of-european-sciences|*The Crisis of European Sciences*]]: [[Galileo]], [[Descartes]], [[Bacon]], [[Newton]] [[self-conscious-bracketing|self-consciously bracketed]] purposes, qualities, and consciousness to make quantifiable scientific progress possible. Over centuries the bracket hardened into a metaphysical claim. That, in a way, whatever was bracketed must not really exist. The methodological move drifted into [[ontological-cover|ontological cover]] for what it made invisible.

The thoughts that resonate: this is the recurring move of every formal apparatus that has trouble admitting what it can't measure; A civilization that has bracketed away purposes and meaning leaves a vacuum; Letting "I can't measure this" slide into "this isn't real."

It's curious that the method Husserl comes up with himself, this [[epoche]], seems to be itself another bracketing method: putting aside questions like *what is real?* and *is mind separated from matter?* so that you can note properties of consciousness you can't access otherwise: [[intentionality]], [[horizon]], [[temporality-husserl|temporality]].

What is also interesting to me is that he goes on this history detective story and comes up with his (in my eyes) quite incomprehensible solution through science and psychology... which makes me think that, again, he pointed out enough for us to understand we have to course correct (the diagnosis of what's wrong is very compelling) but where exactly we should be getting at (for example this method of the epoché) is so unfamiliar that we can barely grasp it. Even Husserl himself seems to struggle.

A [[edge-of-paradigm-gesture|thinker at the edge of a paradigm gestures past it but lacks the vocabulary their successors will develop]]. It's like if Newton late in his life saying things that point toward quantum mechanics — he'd be skipping too many steps for anyone, even himself, to understand what exactly he's getting at. The same way there's no real point in focusing too hard on what Newton would have to say to quantum-like theories, **I struggle to want to engage too much with this epoché theory.** The diagnosis I take. The destination I bracket, I guess.
