The Not-Yet is [[Ernst Bloch]]'s term for the domain of unrealized possibilities that both human [[consciousness]] and the world itself are perpetually oriented toward. It is the horizon of what *could be* but has not yet come into being.

"Even a stone is becoming world." 

This means the Not-Yet is not only subjective (what I imagine could happen) but also objective (what the material conditions of the world are tending toward). [[Anticipatory-consciousness|Anticipatory consciousness]] is the human side of this, but the world has its own latent tendencies as well.

The Not-Yet connects to Bloch's other concepts. The [[darkness-of-the-lived-moment]] exists precisely because each moment is partially constituted by not-yet-realized possibilities. [[Art-as-wish-landscape|Art]] encodes our orientation toward the Not-Yet. And [[cynicism-as-philosophical-suicide-in-bloch|cynicism]] is dangerous because it cuts us off from the Not-Yet entirely, locking us into the present.
