In [[political science]] it is known that some people are "symbolically conservative but operationally liberal”. Meaning they talk like conservatives but want to be governed like liberals. 

But reverse can also be true: voters can be symbolically liberal but operationally conservative.

In practice we know that Black Lives Matter, Kindness Is Everything, and No Human Being Is Illegal. But at home we sit in yards zoned exclusively for single-family homes and in communities that organize against adding the new housing that would bring those stated values closer to reality. 

In conventional political typologies, liberals embrace change and conservatives cling to stasis. But red-state and blue-state [[housing policy]] inverts this completely. In their book they mention [[Texas]] and [[California]] as examples.
