It struck me as faintly absurd: just a few weeks ago I thought of spending tens of euros on ivy. I know people spend *hundreds* building up green coverage and fencing for their gardens. Today I was walking the dog and noticed, I can just step outside, take cuttings, and with very little effort, waiting a few insignificant months, have healthy, growing plants in a few months. 

[[we-do-more-because-we-can|We do more just because we can]], I guess, and needs to be now if we can too.

[[we-just-burned-it|Burning what's abundant]] and *buying* what you can grow for free.
