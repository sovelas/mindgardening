## `beauvoir/` 
- [[ambiguity-of-human-existence]] — Human existence is fundamentally ambiguous: we live between multiple unresolvable dualities simultaneously

- [[why-the-adventurer-fails]] — The adventurer denies the individual/collective duality and ignores the web of freedom sustaining them
- [[the-aesthetic-attitude]] — The ideal of encountering art through detached, disinterested perception, as if one could suspend bias and see the work “as it is”

- [[the-aesthetic-attitude-and-scientific-inquiry]] — The aesthetic attitude resembles scientific inquiry in its attempt to suspend bias, but becomes dangerous when detachment hardens into false objectivity and evasion of responsibility
