Free association: instead of being forced to participate in the society you happen to be born into, the only communities you belong to are ones you voluntarily choose. These are groups of like-minded people with similar goals, where your cooperation genuinely benefits you and their cooperation genuinely benefits them.
What anarchists call [[solidarity]].

This principle challenges the default assumption that membership in a nation-state, or submission to a particular employer or institution, should be compulsory. If a group stops serving your interests, you should have the [[liberty]] to leave and join another community at any time.

This voluntary structure serves as a built-in check on [[power]]. Any individual who believes a group has been corrupted (by drift from its original purpose) retains the freedom to exit (bye bye job). 

Communities must then continually earn the participation of their members rather than relying on coercion or the inability to leave. This is the social building block upon which the larger [[decentralized-federated-network]] is constructed.
