[[simone-weil|Weil's]] points out that [[attention-as-sacrament|attention]] (the capacity to truly perceive another person) is what breaks the spell of [[affliction]] (spiritual malaise, hopelessness). [[muskism-as-governing-philosophy|Musk's]] DOGE project arrives at the precise inversion: [[empathy-as-software-exploit|empathy is a bug in Western civilization]] that must be patched.

For Weil, [[force-in-human-events|force]] operates by turning persons into things, and the only counterforce is the moment when someone stops, attends, and sees the person inside the instrument. The [[people-reduced-to-things|salesman who sees you as a commission]] is force operating in the everyday; the [[person-in-a-ditch-metaphor|person who stops for the stranger in a ditch]] is attention interrupting force. 

Attention is costly, it requires [[passive-activity-and-negative-effort|negative effort]], the discipline of emptying oneself rather than projecting.
Attention is also fragile, always at risk of being overwhelmed by the gravitational pull towar...instrumentalization. 
But it is what can make us perceive a person *as* a person rather than as a resource.

Where Weil sees attention to suffering as the highest human capacity, Musk sees it as a vulnerability. A social engineering attack against which rational systems must build defenses. Where Weil insists that [[force-in-human-events|force]] dehumanizes by turning people into instruments, the [[state-as-codebase|state-as-codebase]] *begins* from the premise that people are already data entries whose validity is the only question. The [[doge-data-integration-and-the-surveillance-state|"God View"]] with all taxpayer data unified in a single portal, is an opposite of Weil's attention: where attention requires emptying the self to receive the other, the God View requires total informational capture so that no other can escape classification.

[[byung-chul-han|Han's]] [[psychopolitics|psychopolitics]], as some sort of intermediary, shows that [[positive-vs-negative-power|positive power]] works by making domination feel like freedom, and that in the [[achievement-society]] the capacity for genuine encounter with the [[the-other-and-negativity|Other]] has already atrophied and been replaced by [[illusory-engagement-with-difference|narcissistic self-affirmation disguised as engagement]]. 

A society that has already lost the practice of attention does not even experience the declaration that empathy is a bug as monstrous. It experiences it as a frank acknowledgment of what everyone already implicitly accepts: that other people are either useful or they are overhead. Yuck. 
The DOGE architects did not need to *destroy* attention. The [[achievement-society|achievement society]] had already done half the work for them.
