Does it matter who *decides* what I care about? Does it matter who sets the standards I strive for? Literally — does it? I'll probably strive for them regardless of where they came from. And does it matter whether I do it for myself, or out of a will to enlarge other people's capabilities/ rights/ standards of living.

The worry underneath is [[shaped-preference-of-roots|shaped preference]]: my cares didn't fall from the sky, they were trained into me, so "what I authentically want" might just be a record of who got to me first. And introspecting harder won't dig past it — you can't audit your wants using the very wants under audit.

But maybe the *origin* of a care matters less than its *grip* and its *direction*. A care I didn't choose can still be tested by what it does: does it widen the world or narrow it, does it survive [[the-little-voice|the little voice I'm meant to keep honest]], does it reach past my own comfort toward [[moving-standards|someone else's standing]]? Provenance is unprovable; consequences aren't. So maybe I better judge the care by where it points, not by where it came from.
