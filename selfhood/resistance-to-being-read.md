Why is there such a tight resistance to sharing my thoughts with anyone in the first place? I keep flinching — *who cares*. Is it really because no one does care? Or because if I never show the thought, I never have to find out?

A garden like this is visible but easy to look away from.

[[internalized-shame|The flinch has the shape of shame]] and shame's oldest trick is that it disguises itself as modesty. But there's also a damage of not-saying. So I keep writing it out anyway, and leaving the gate open, even while flinching.
