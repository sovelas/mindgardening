Why is there such a tight resistance to sharing my thoughts with anyone in the first place? I keep flinching — *who cares*. Is it really because no one does care? Or because if I never show the thought, I never have to find out?

A garden like this is visible but easy to look away from.

[[shame-internalized-as-identity|The flinch has the shape of shame]] — not "I did something bad" but "I *am* too much, or not enough, to be seen" — and shame's oldest trick is that it disguises itself as modesty. *Who's to say if I flinch — there'll be no damage done* is the consoling lie. The damage is the not-saying: a thought you never put down stays a feeling, and a thought you never show stays unfinished. So I keep writing it out anyway, and leaving the gate open, even while flinching. See also [[from-feeling-to-synthesis]].
