According to [[emil-cioran|Cioran]] when every conventional reason for living (moral, aesthetic, religious, social, etc) is stripped away, what remains is the void: [[nothingness]]. 

So, how can one sustain life without succumbing to that void? 
His answer is not to fill it with meaning but to connect with the absurd itself, to cultivate what he called a [[love of absolute uselessness]], an affection for existence even after giving up the demand that it be justified. Loving something which does not have substance but which simulates an illusion of life. Holding on to passing intensities, even though you know they might not be "true".
